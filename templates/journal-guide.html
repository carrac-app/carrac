Trade Journal —
Step-by-Step Guide

The trade journal has two interfaces: a sidebar panel on the dashboard (for quick actions next to the chart) and a dedicated page at /journal (for review and analytics). This guide walks through every workflow step by step.

Where to find things

Sidebar Click the Journal button in the right panel's view mode strip (dashboard page). Best for: logging trades while charting, checking open positions, quick closes.

Full Page Navigate to /journal from the sidebar's "Full Journal" link or the header menu. Best for: reviewing trades, analytics, calendar, sessions, playbooks, goals, AI review.

1. Logging a New Trade

From the sidebar Sidebar

1
Switch to Journal view
Click the Journal button in the right panel's vertical strip. The panel shows your stats, open trades, and recent closed trades.
2
Click "+ Log Trade"
An inline form appears with the current chart asset pre-filled. Enter your entry price, stop loss, take profit, direction, and setup type.
3
Click "Open Trade"
The trade is created and appears in the Open Trades section. You can keep charting — the trade is next to you.
ⓘ You can also create trades from chart drawings. Draw a position overlay on the chart (press B), then click "Import Drawings" in the sidebar to auto-create journal entries from your drawn levels.

From the full page Full Page

1
Click "+ Log Trade"
A modal opens with all fields: asset, timeframe, direction, entry, stop, target, size, fees, setup type, confidence, mood, notes, tags, and pre-trade checklist.
2
Fill in the details and save
Required: asset and entry price. Everything else is optional but more data = better analytics.
2. Closing a Trade

From the sidebar Sidebar

1
Find the trade in Open Trades
Your open trades are listed with entry price and TP. The "This Asset" section at the top highlights trades for the asset you're currently charting.
2
Click "Close"
An inline form expands. Enter exit price, select exit reason (TP hit, SL hit, manual, etc.), and optionally add close notes.

From the full page Full Page

1
Find the trade and click "Close"
A modal opens showing trade context (direction, asset, entry, SL, TP) at the top. Enter exit price, exit reason, select mistake tags, and add notes.
ⓘ R-multiple, MFE/MAE, and exit efficiency are calculated automatically from your entry, stop, and exit prices.
3. Editing a Trade

Both Click on any trade card in the sidebar to expand an edit form, or click the Edit button on the full page. You can modify entry/stop/target prices, direction, setup type, notes, tags, and more. Changes are saved instantly.

4. Reviewing Your Trades

Trade details Full Page

1
Click "Details" on any trade
Expands a panel showing notes, checklist results, tags, MFE/MAE, exit efficiency, AI review, grade, screenshots, and trade metadata.

AI review Full Page

1
Click "Generate AI Review" in trade details
The AI analyzes your trade using both your journal data and the market context at entry and exit — the 12-point scanner score (trend, momentum, RSI, structure, level, macro), market regime, Fear & Greed index, and setup classification. It grades setup quality, execution, exit timing, and discipline, then points out patterns in how you planned, entered and exited. The review compares entry vs exit conditions to assess whether your exit was justified by changing market data. Uses AI credits. You can regenerate anytime.

Calendar view Full Page

1
Switch to the Calendar tab
A monthly heatmap shows your daily P&L in R-multiples. Green = profitable day, red = losing day. Click any day to see its trades.
5. Analytics

Sidebar analytics Sidebar

Click Analytics in the journal sidebar to expand key metrics: expectancy, profit factor, average hold time, win/loss streaks, and breakdowns by setup/asset/direction/timeframe/weekday. Also shows monthly equity bars and Kelly position sizing.

Full analytics Full Page

The Analytics tab on the full page adds:

•
Equity curve
Canvas-drawn cumulative R over time with drawdown shading. Select 30d/90d/180d/1y range.
•
R-multiple histogram
Distribution of your trade outcomes in R. Shows your average R with a dashed line.
•
MFE/MAE scatter plot
Maximum Favorable vs Adverse Excursion for each trade. Reveals if you're leaving profit on the table or letting losers run.
6. Sessions

Full Page Sessions track your trading day structure.

1
Create a session before trading
Click "+ New Session" in the Sessions tab. Write your pre-session plan: market outlook, focus areas, rules to follow.
2
Trade normally
Log and close trades as usual. They're automatically linked to the session by date.
3
Review the session after trading
Edit the session and fill in post-session notes. What went well? What to improve? Click "AI Summary" to get an AI-generated debrief based on your actual trades.
7. Playbooks & Goals

Full Page Both live under the Playbooks tab.

Playbooks

Document your trading setups. Each playbook has a name, setup type, description, ideal conditions, entry rules, and exit rules. When you tag trades with the same setup type, the playbook shows stats (trades, win rate, net R, avg R) automatically.

Monthly goals

Set targets for each month: max daily loss, max trades/day, min R:R target, max consecutive losses before stopping. Progress bars show where you stand. Green = within target, yellow = close, red = breached.

8. Kelly Position Sizing

Sidebar Available in the sidebar analytics panel.

1
Enter your account size
The calculator uses your actual win rate and avg win/loss from your trade history.
2
Select Kelly fraction
15% (safe), 25% (standard), or 50% (aggressive). This is the fraction of Kelly optimal to use — full Kelly is too volatile for real trading.
3
Enter entry and stop for your current trade
The calculator tells you the exact position size in dollars and units based on your Kelly-derived risk per trade.
9. AI Features

AI features use credits (shown in the header on the full page). Rate limit: 10 calls per minute.

FeatureWhereWhat it does
Trade review Full page (trade details) AI analyzes a single trade using market context at entry and exit — scanner score, regime, Fear & Greed, setup classification. Grades setup, execution, exit, discipline
Session summary Full page (sessions) AI reviews all trades in a session with per-trade market context. Shows dominant regime, average entry score, and whether trades aligned with conditions
Weekly review Sidebar + full page AI summary with performance-by-regime and performance-by-entry-score correlation — shows which market conditions you trade best in
NL query Full page (analytics) Ask questions in plain English — now includes market context data. Try "win rate when regime was Risk-Off" or "show trades where entry verdict was STRONG"
10. Import & Export

Import from chart drawings

Sidebar Click "Import Drawings". Any position overlays drawn on charts (press B on the chart) are converted to journal trades with entry, stop, target, and direction pre-filled.

Export

Full Page Two options in the Playbooks tab:

•
Export Trades CSV
All trades (including open) as a CSV file. Good for spreadsheet analysis.
•
Export All JSON
Full backup: trades, sessions, playbooks, goals. Can be used for migration or backup.
11. This Asset (Sidebar)

Sidebar When you switch to Journal view, the top section shows your trade history for the asset currently on the chart. Navigate to BTC and you see your BTC trades. Switch to ETH and it updates automatically. This lets you see your track record on an asset while analyzing it.
