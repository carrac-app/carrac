Frame · Plays

Stop trading ideas you never defined.

A Play is a structured thesis with conditions and invalidation — not a sticky note you rewrite later. Conditions auto-evaluate in the background.

  • Thesis — your actual reasoning, long-form
  • Conditions — formula-driven, auto-evaluated, all / any / custom logic
  • Invalidation — set at creation, captured forever
  • Status machine — watching → acting → completed / expired / invalidated
  • Warning when a condition reverses post-entry
