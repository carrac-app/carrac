3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not use third-party tracking cookies (no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel)
  • We never see or store your card or bank details — payment is handled entirely by our payment provider
  • Exchange API keys are collected only if you explicitly connect an exchange, are stored encrypted, and are only ever used to read your trade history. At connection time we verify the key is read-only and refuse any key with trading or withdrawal permissions
  • We do not track your browsing activity outside of the Platform
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing

4. Legal Basis (GDPR Article 6)

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)): Account data, user content, exchange connections, billing state — necessary to provide the service you signed up for
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): Error tracking, security logs, usage/cost records, sign-up attestations — necessary to keep the Platform secure, functional and accountable
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): In-app product analytics — loads only after you accept the consent banner and can be withdrawn at any time

5. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate you and maintain your session
  • Store and display your drawings, plays, journal entries, and preferences
  • Import your trade history from exchanges you have connected
  • Generate AI reports that reference your active plays
  • Send you price alerts and notifications (when you opt in)
  • Monitor Platform health and fix bugs
  • Protect against brute-force attacks and abuse

6. Data Storage and Security

  • Hosting: A hosting provider in Germany. Server-side only — no client-side databases
  • Database: PostgreSQL, reachable only from the application's private container network on the same host — it is not exposed to the internet
  • Passwords: Hashed with Argon2 (never stored in plaintext)
  • Exchange API keys: Stored encrypted; decrypted only at the moment a sync runs
  • Transport: HTTPS enforced via HSTS (preload), TLS 1.2+
  • Headers: CSP, X-Content-Type-Options, X-Frame-Options, Referrer-Policy
  • Sessions: Server-side Redis with Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax cookies
  • Backups: Nightly database backups, replicated to infrastructure we operate inside the EU, retained on a rolling schedule of up to roughly three months

It is linked to your account through a one-way identifier rather than your account id or email, and it is deleted automatically after 90 days.

9. Your Rights (GDPR Articles 15–22)

As an EU-based service, you have the following rights:

  • Access & portability (Art. 15, 20): Export everything we hold about you yourself, any time — Settings → Danger Zone → Export. The export is machine-readable JSON and free on every plan.
  • Erasure (Art. 17): Delete your account yourself — Settings → Danger Zone → Delete account. This removes or anonymises your data across our database immediately, including exchange connections and assistant conversation history. If any part of the deletion cannot complete, you are told, not left to assume. Copies held by the processors above (analytics, email) are removed as a follow-up step, and deleted records age out of backups within roughly three months.
  • Rectification (Art. 16): Some details (such as your trader profile) you can edit in Settings; for anything you cannot edit yourself — including your email address or display name — write to us and we will correct it.
  • Restriction (Art. 18): Request that we limit processing of your data
  • Objection (Art. 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interest

If you joined the early-access list without ever creating an account, there is no account to delete — email us and we will remove you from the list, including the operational copy of it.

To exercise any of these rights, email {{ support_email }}. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

10. Cookies & consent

Carrac stores two categories of client-side data:

  • Strictly necessary: a server-side session cookie for authentication, a CSRF token, and a small set of localStorage entries that hold your in-app preferences (chosen view, last-viewed asset, etc.). These cannot be disabled while using the Platform.
  • Analytics & product-improvement (consent-gated in the app): when you accept the consent banner inside the app we initialise PostHog product analytics (page views and product events). We do not record your screen — session replay is disabled. You can withdraw consent at any time from the Cookie preferences link, after which PostHog stops loading and existing person data can be deleted on request.

On the public pages (landing, this page's siblings) we measure visits with a cookieless configuration: nothing is written to your device and no identifier persists beyond the page view.

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party trackers, or any non-essential cookies that you have not consented to.

11. Children

Carrac is not intended for anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

12. International Data Transfers

Your account and everything you create on the Platform are stored on servers in the European Union, and our analytics stay in the EU too. A few of the services listed above operate outside it — in particular Anthropic and xAI in the United States (report generation), Cloudflare's global network, and the font and script CDNs. Where personal data leaves the EU, the transfer is covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the equivalent UK provisions, which hold those providers to the same standard of protection. Cloudflare may cache static files on servers worldwide; those contain no personal data. If you connect your own AI client over our API or the Model Context Protocol, anything you choose to send goes to the provider you picked, under your own agreement with them.
