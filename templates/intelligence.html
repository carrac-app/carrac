Derivatives Regime Engine
12Deterministic regimes

Same setup. Different regime. Different outcome.

Every morning, Carrac computes a derivatives fingerprint from perpetual-futures positioning — funding, open interest and long/short balance — then matches it to one of 12 regimes. Fingerprints are auditable rules — not ML confidence numbers.

  • Basis / APR (annualized futures premium)
  • 25Δ skew (put IV − call IV)
  • IV term structure: 7d / 30d / 90d slope
  • GEX net + flip point
  • IV − RV premium (implied minus realized)
Context changes how a setup behaves. The same logic can look very different in a Healthy Trend than in a Euphoric Melt-Up — which is why Carrac lets you slice your own results by regime.
