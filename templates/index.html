Carrac — User Guide

            
Multi-timeframe technical analysis dashboard for swing and position traders. 337 curated assets plus dynamic symbol search across crypto, stocks, ETFs, indices, forex, and commodities. Real-time data via Binance WebSocket.


            

                
                
            


            
Getting Started

            
Morning routine (5-10 min):

            
    
                
  1. Glance at the sentiment bar (F&G — Fear & Greed index, MVRV-Z — BTC valuation vs. cost basis, Funding Rate — perpetuals long/short cost) for macro mood
    2. 
                
  2. Open Morning Report — 8-tab analysis: Summary, Macro, Alerts, Social, Relative Strength, Assets, Options, Internals
    3. 
                
  3. Switch to Watchlist — scan signal badges (SQZ — EMA squeeze, flip imminent; V-CPR — price vacuum, may pull to the middle; TF-DIV — daily disagrees with higher timeframes; CC — Consensio changed since yesterday) for overnight changes
    4. 
                
  4. Click any flagged asset → chart opens → quick timeframe scan (1D → 1W)
    5. 
                
  5. Press Q to cycle to AI panel → click "Analyze" for a second opinion
    6. 
            

            
Finding setups:

            
    
                
  1. Sort by Consensio (multi-MA alignment score, out of 9) — find 7-9/9 (strong bull) or 0-2/9 (strong bear) extremes
    2. 
                
  2. Check EMA Ribbon (two EMAs colored by trend) column for SQZ/TGT badges (TGT — EMAs converging, flip may be imminent)
    3. 
                
  3. Open chart → toggle indicators: CPR + EMA Ribbon + RSI Divergence
    4. 
                
  4. Cycle timeframes (, / .) to confirm multi-TF alignment
    5. 
                
  5. Create a play (P) → AI Validate for structured scorecard
    6. 
            


            
Backtester

            
Open the Backtest tab in the chart drawer to test a rule against the asset’s own history before you act on it. Write an entry and an exit formula — the same expression language as custom indicators — and Run. Trades are marked on the chart, and the results report win rate, profit factor, drawdown and Sharpe.

            
    
                
  • Regime filter — split the results by the market regime in force at the time, so you can see whether an edge is real or just one long trending stretch.
    • 
                
  • Realism & Risk Controls — collapsed by default. Slippage, stops, targets and fill timing all live here. A stop set while Stop Type is on Close only does nothing — switch it to Intrabar.
    • 
                
  • Save / Load keeps the formulas and costs, but not the timeframe, date range or risk controls. History lists past runs, including any your AI assistant saved over MCP.
    • 
            

            
Full detail in the Backtester guide.


            
Layout & Navigation

            
Split layout: chart on the left, panel on the right with a draggable divider.

            
View modes (press E to cycle, Shift+E backward):

            
    
                
  • Lite — Minimal screener (symbol, price, change, consensio)
    • 
                
  • Consensio — Default view with Consensio, EMA Ribbon, signals
    • 
                
  • Detail — Full screener with all columns and signal badges
    • 
                
  • Report — Morning analysis report inline
    • 
                
  • AI — AI assistant with chat and action presets
    • 
                
  • Journal — Quick trade journal panel
    • 
                
  • Collapsed — Panel hidden, chart takes full width
    • 
            

            
Panel tabs:

            
    
                
  • Watchlist — Personal symbol list (star to add/remove, drag to reorder)
    • 
                
  • Crypto — All curated cryptocurrency assets
    • 
                
  • TradFi — Stocks, ETFs, indices, forex, commodities
    • 
                
  • All — Complete catalog across all categories
    • 
            


            
Sentiment Bar

            
Header indicators for quick market read (hover for tooltips):

            
    
                
  • Regime — Market regime badge (Risk-On / Neutral / Risk-Off)
    • 
                
  • F&G — Fear & Greed Index (0-100). 0-24 Extreme Fear, 25-44 Fear, 45-54 Neutral, 55-74 Greed, 75-100 Extreme Greed
    • 
                
  • MVRV-Z — On-chain valuation. <0 undervalued, 0-3 fair, 3-7 expensive, >7 overvalued
    • 
                
  • ASI — Alt Season Index. <25 BTC season, 25-75 neutral, >75 alt season
    • 
                
  • L/S R — Long/Short Ratio (Binance futures)
    • 
                
  • FR — Funding Rate. Positive = bullish crowd, negative = bearish crowd
    • 
                
  • OI — Open Interest change (24h)
    • 
            


            
Screener Columns

            
    
                
  • Consensio (X/9) — Multi-TF MA alignment. 9/9 = full bull, 0/9 = full bear
    • 
                
  • 1D / 3D / 1W — Per-timeframe signal strength (-3 to +3). Green flash = improved, red = dropped since yesterday
    • 
                
  • W-RSI — Weekly RSI (14). Below 30 = oversold, above 70 = overbought
    • 
                
  • CPR — Monthly Central Pivot Range zone. S3–S1, BC–TC (inside), R1–R3
    • 
                
  • BB — Bollinger Band squeeze. <30% extreme, 30-50% squeeze, 50-150% normal
    • 
                
  • EMA Ribbon — EMA 30/60 crossover. Green = above, red = below, yellow = inside
    • 
                
  • Changed — Days since the EMA Ribbon trend last flipped
    • 
                
  • Structure — Daily market structure. HH/HL uptrend, LH/LL downtrend
    • 
                
  • ADX — Trend strength. <20 ranging, 20-30 forming, >30 strong
    • 
            


            
Signal Badges

            
Compact badges in the Signals column. Hover any badge for details.

            
    
                
  • SQZ — EMA Squeeze — 30/60 EMAs within 0.5%, flip imminent
    • 
                
  • TGT — Tightening — EMAs converging, early warning before SQZ
    • 
                
  • V-CPR — CPR Vacuum — price hasn't touched monthly CPR zone yet
    • 
                
  • S3 / R3 — Near monthly S3 support / R3 resistance (within 2%)
    • 
                
  • TF-DIV — Daily vs weekly direction disagree
    • 
                
  • RSI↑ / RSI↓ — RSI bullish / bearish divergence
    • 
                
  • H-RSI↑ / H-RSI↓ — Hidden divergences (trend continuation)
    • 
                
  • STR↑ / STR↓ — Structure disagrees with EMA Ribbon trend
    • 
                
  • CC — Consensio changed since yesterday
    • 
            


            
Chart & Indicators

            
Click any asset name to open the chart. Crypto: 11 timeframes (1m to 1M). TradFi: 1H, 4H, 1D, 3D, 1W, 1M. Live prices via Binance WebSocket.

            
Indicator categories (press I to toggle panel):

            
    
                
  • Oscillators: RSI (TradingView-style with MA line), MACD, TTM Squeeze, BB Width %, ATR %, NR7
    • 
                
  • Trend & MAs: Daily EMAs (21/50/200), Weekly MAs, Bull Market Band, EMA Ribbon, Consensio, Alligator, CM Ultimate, Keltner Tight, Pi Cycle Top, SMA 20/200
    • 
                
  • Levels & Zones: CPR + CPR History, Key Levels, S/R Channels, Auto Fib, Fair Value Gaps, Liquidation Zones
    • 
                
  • Volume & Flow: Volume, Volume Profile, CVD Spot, CVD Futures (USDT/COIN), Open Interest, Derivatives Combined
    • 
                
  • Structure: Market Structure, Order Blocks, Equal H/L, Value Zones
    • 
            

            
Indicator Groups: Click "+" in the favourite pills bar to create a named preset of indicators. Click a group pill to toggle all members on/off. Right-click for rename/edit/delete.

            
Log scale: Press O or click the A/L buttons on the Y-axis. All indicators, drawings, and live updates work correctly in both modes.


            
Drawing Tools

            
Full toolbar with all drawing types. Drawings sync across devices via server.

            
    
                
  • L — Trendline. P — Parallel channel. R — Rectangle (with corner/edge resize handles). F — Fibonacci retracement
    • 
                
  • H — Horizontal line. B — Position tool (entry/SL/TP with R:R). T — Text annotation
    • 
                
  • X — Measure tool. Shift+V — Volume Profile range. Shift+A — Alert line
    • 
                
  • C — Cycle colors. +/- — Thickness. [/] — Opacity. Del — Delete selected
    • 
                
  • Ctrl+Z/Y — Undo/redo. D — Toggle toolbar. Sub-pane drawing supported (RSI, MACD, etc.)
    • 
            

            
Spot Buy Tracker: Click the teal Spot button, place entries on the chart. Each shows as a triangle marker. A dashed average entry line shows aggregate cost basis with live P&L. Full portfolio tree view in the Positions drawer tab.


            
Price Alerts

            
    
                
  • Press A to open alerts panel. Click the chart to set the price level
    • 
                
  • Auto-detects above/below direction from current price
    • 
                
  • Notified via ntfy push notifications. Premium: Telegram + Discord
    • 
                
  • Free: 5 active alerts. Premium: 100
    • 
                
  • Frequency: once per bar or once (auto-disable). Editing a triggered alert reactivates it
    • 
                
  • Alert lines visible as overlays on the chart
    • 
            


            
Plays

            
    
                
  • Thesis-first trade hypotheses with structured conditions, milestones, and invalidation tracking
    • 
                
  • Press P to open plays panel. Create plays with entry/exit zones, conviction levels, and condition checklists
    • 
                
  • Condition builder: 17 condition types across 6 categories with ALL/ANY/custom logic
    • 
                
  • Auto-evaluated against live market data. Create alerts from play conditions. Log to journal on completion
    • 
                
  • Plays visible in morning reports with per-condition evidence
    • 
                
  • Free: 3 plays. Premium: 10
    • 
            


            
Positions Tracker

            
Card-based position tracker in the bottom drawer Positions tab.

            
    
                
  • Each card shows direction, entry, SL, TP levels, per-target R:R ratios, and live P&L
    • 
                
  • Cards glow green in profit, red in loss. Dashed indicator shows where price sits vs targets
    • 
                
  • Drag SL/entry/TP levels directly on the chart to reposition. Click a card to center the chart on it
    • 
                
  • Hover actions: Validate (AI scorecard), Close & Log (to journal), Clear (remove from chart), Delete
    • 
                
  • Toggle "All assets" to see positions across your entire portfolio
    • 
                
  • Futures/Spots/Both toggle — spot buys show as a hierarchical tree with aggregate P&L
    • 
            


            
Scanner

            
    
                
  • 6 conditions checked per watchlist asset, 2 points each (0-12 scale)
    • 
                
  • Criteria: Consensio trend, EMA Ribbon+MACD momentum, RSI positioning, swing structure, S/R proximity, macro regime
    • 
                
  • Labels: High (11-12), Moderate (8-10), Mixed (6-7), Low (3-5), Conflicting (0-2)
    • 
                
  • Press S to open scanner tab. Click to expand detail, double-click to navigate to chart
    • 
                
  • Filters by alignment label and direction (long/short/both). Auto-refreshes every 60s
    • 
                
  • Score snapshots captured at trade open and close, shown in the journal
    • 
            


            
AI Assistant

            
AI-powered analysis using Grok. Access via the AI view mode (E to cycle) or directly in the panel.

            
Action presets (click "+" dropdown):

            
    
                
  • Analyze — Full chart read: trend, key levels, indicators, notable observations
    • 
                
  • Validate — 0-12 scorecard across 6 criteria with a High/Moderate/Mixed/Low/Conflicting label, plus alternative setups
    • 
                
  • Challenge — Devil's advocate — every reason the trade could fail
    • 
                
  • Checklist — 9-point pre-trade evaluation (PASS/WARN/FAIL per criterion)
    • 
                
  • Confluence — Multi-timeframe alignment analysis (1D vs 3D vs 1W)
    • 
                
  • Patterns — Shape correlation search (Pearson r ≥ 0.85) across 1D/3D/1W with sparkline overlays and forward returns
    • 
            

            
Conversations persist across sessions (50 messages). History dropdown to load past chats. Font size adjustable (A-/A+).

            
Trading Profile: 40+ field identity (11 sections) in Settings. Covers trading style, risk management, psychology, market biases. Profile context is injected into every AI response for personalized analysis.


            
Trade Journal

            
Full trading journal at /journal (dedicated page) or as a panel view mode. Also accessible from the hamburger menu.

            
    
                
  • Log trades — Asset, direction, entry/SL/TP, size, fees, setup type, confidence & mood sliders, tags. Live R:R preview as you type
    • 
                
  • Close trades — Exit price, reason (Manual/TP/SL/Trailing/Time/BE), mistake tags (FOMO, revenge, etc.), MFE/MAE auto-calculated
    • 
                
  • Trade list — Sortable table with filters (date range, asset, direction, setup, mistakes). KPI bar with win rate, avg R, best/worst
    • 
                
  • Equity curve — Cumulative R over time with drawdown shading
    • 
                
  • Calendar — Monthly grid showing daily net R, trade count, wins/losses
    • 
                
  • Sessions — Group trades by day with pre/post notes. AI session summary
    • 
                
  • Analytics — R-multiple histogram, MFE/MAE scatter, breakdowns by setup/asset/direction/weekday/duration, rule adherence tracking, streak stats, NL query for custom analysis
    • 
                
  • Playbooks — Document setups with rules and conditions. Live stats from matching trades
    • 
                
  • Goals — Monthly targets (max daily loss, max trades/day, min R:R). Color-coded progress bars
    • 
                
  • AI review — Entry quality, exit timing, risk management grades per trade. Weekly summary with action items
    • 
                
  • Setup checklists — Template per setup type, auto-renders in trade modal, analytics track adherence vs performance
    • 
                
  • Screenshots — Auto-captured at open and close, visible in trade detail
    • 
                
  • Export — CSV (trades) or JSON (full backup including sessions, playbooks, goals)
    • 
            


            
Morning Report

            
AI-generated daily analysis. Access via hamburger menu or Report view mode.

            
    
                
  • 8 tabs: Summary, Macro, Alerts, Social, Relative Strength, Assets, Options, Internals
    • 
                
  • Voice briefing — 2-3 minute spoken summary delivered via ntfy and Telegram
    • 
                
  • Asset ticker mentions are clickable — navigate directly to the chart
    • 
                
  • Keyboard: J/K cycles tabs, 1-8 direct jump. PDF export available
    • 
            

            
Personal sections (Premium): Executive Summary, X Feed Digest, YouTube Analyst Insights, Devil's Advocate on positions, Play Evaluations, Alerts & Positions Follow-Up, Comparison to Yesterday, Pattern Status, Action Items.

            
Analyst sources: Configure your X handles and YouTube channels in Settings → Analyst Sources for personalized report sections.


            
Multi-Chart

            
View up to 4 charts simultaneously. Each pane has independent asset and timeframe.

            
    
                
  • Layouts: Single, Dual (side-by-side), Tri (1+2 stack), Quad (2×2 grid)
    • 
                
  • Ctrl+1/2/3/4/5 — Switch layouts. Tab/Shift+Tab — Cycle active pane
    • 
                
  • Active pane gets blue border and receives all keyboard shortcuts
    • 
                
  • Companion panes auto-open at the next timeframe up from the primary
    • 
            

            
Rotation mode:

            
    
                
  • Ctrl+6 — Rotate through all plays. Ctrl+7 — Rotate through watchlist
    • 
                
  • Adjustable speed (5s to 5m). Pause/resume, manual navigation. Screen stays awake
    • 
                
  • Full-screen rotation hides all panels for maximum chart space. ESC to exit
    •
